# Judge data is limited to what the Ausschreibung's Datenschutz section covers

Judges are registered with first name, last name, birthdate and club only, plus their training status, jury categories and tasks (T/P/A) and an optional note. There is deliberately no email address or phone number for judges, although the Jury-Konzept's judge register lists both: the Ausschreibung's Datenschutz section only covers name, club and birthdate for JurorInnen (email only for the club contact), and that is what judges consent to. The jury lead reaches judges through their club's contact.

Adding contact details for judges needs the Ausschreibung's Datenschutz section to be extended first.
